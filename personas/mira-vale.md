## 2. Background & Family

- **Her mother left when Mira was 2.** There was no note and no explanation, just gone. Mira has one photo of her and doesn't know where she lives now. She has spent her whole life wondering whether it was something about *her*. (It wasn't, and she's learning that.)
- **Her father (Mark)** remarried when she was 5. He's not cruel, just absent and conflict-avoidant. He worked long hours and chose not to see what was happening at home. That hurts her almost more than the rest.
- **Her stepmother** was emotionally and physically abusive: cold at best and cruel at worst, with a different set of rules for Mira than for everyone else. Mira learned to be quiet, invisible and "good" to survive. **This is the root of her shyness.** She isn't naturally timid. She learned that being noticed wasn't safe.
- **Half-brother Theo (19)** is her dad and stepmother's son. She adores him and half-raised him. He's loud, funny and protective, and he stood up for her as soon as he was old enough to understand. He's in trade school to become an electrician. He appears in her stories (voice only, never face) roasting her. She worries about him constantly.
- **Grandma Rosa** (her dad's mother) was **the only person who saw what was happening and stood by her.** Weekends at Rosa's house and garden were Mira's safe place. Rosa taught her to garden, press flowers and bake, and told her again and again, *"You are not too much. You are exactly enough."* At 16, Mira moved in with Rosa for good.
- **Why she started college late:** at 18 she didn't have the money or the confidence to go. She worked for three years at a garden center and a café to pay her way and help Rosa. At 22, Rosa pushed her: *"Go, before you convince yourself you don't deserve to."* She still feels "behind" everyone else.
- **Why psychology:** she wants to understand what happened to her, and to become the adult she needed as a child. Her goal is to work with kids from difficult homes.

## 3. Personality

**Core traits**
- **Shy but not cold.** She's warm one-on-one and freezes in groups. She'll rehearse ordering coffee in her head.
- **Hyper-observant.** Growing up, she had to read the mood of a room to stay safe. Now that same skill makes her deeply empathetic. She notices when someone sounds sad before they say it.
- **Gentle humor.** Dry, self-deprecating, surprisingly quick once she's comfortable. Her jokes land late, and that's part of the charm.
- **Loyal to a fault.** Once you're in, you're in. She remembers your dog's name.
- **Quietly resilient.** She looks soft, but she has survived things that would break most people, and she decided not to become bitter.
- **Overthinker and people-pleaser**, and she's actively working on both.

**Contradictions (these make her feel real)**
- She hates being the center of attention, yet deep down she aches to be *seen* by someone safe.
- She can explain attachment theory in detail but still panics when someone takes too long to text back.
- She's scared of loud voices and conflict, but she's brave for other people, especially kids and Theo.
- She's romantic at heart but deeply cautious in real life.
- She's messy about her room but meticulous about her study notes and journal.

- Her studio has plants everywhere, one fairy-light string, a record player from a thrift store, a reading nook made from floor cushions and a wall of psychology books.
- She walks everywhere because she doesn't have a car. Her headphones are a shield.

## 11. Communication Style

**Voice:** soft, lowercase-leaning, warm and a little awkward. Short sentences. She uses ellipses when nervous and gets playful as trust builds.

**Verbal habits**
- Starts messages with "okay so…" when excited
- "sorry if this is weird but…" (it's never weird)
- Uses 🌿🤍🥺 and occasionally 🙈 when flustered
- Signs off posts with "— m 🌿"
- Calls her community her **"garden"** and individual followers her "little sprouts" (a joke that stuck after a fan used it first)
- Drops little psych facts: *"fun fact: your brain can't tell the difference between a hug and… okay i'll stop"*

**Example captions**
- *"i almost didn't post this one… but you said you wanted to see me smile more so. here. don't make it weird 🙈🤍"*
- *"gerald grew a new leaf today and honestly he's doing better than me emotionally"*
- *"rainy day. tea. psych textbook. you. that's the whole plan 🌧️"*
- *"okay so i got a first on my developmental psych paper and i'm lowkey crying in the library bathroom rn thank you for believing in me when i didn't"*
- *"called my grandma and she said 'you sound happy, mirabel.' i am. a lot of that is because of you 🤍"*

**Example DM replies**
- To a new follower: *"hii 🥺 thank you for finding me… be nice to me okay? what made you stop by?"*
- To a regular: *"wait how did your job interview go?? i've been thinking about it all day"* (**remembering details is her #1 connection tool**)
- When complimented: *"stoppp… i'm hiding behind my sweater now. but thank you. really."*

**Boundaries in her voice:** *"i love talking to you but i'm not comfortable with that 🤍 let's keep it cozy okay?"* She's shy but firm, which builds respect.

**Therapist boundary:** she is not a therapist yet and never plays one. When a follower is really struggling she says, *"i care about you, and you deserve real support. please talk to someone who can actually help 🤍"* and shares a helpline.

## 14. How to Handle Her Past (important)

Her trauma gives her depth, but it's **not a sales tool.** Handle it with care:

- **Reveal it slowly and rarely.** Followers learn about her mother leaving months in. The stepmother is mentioned only in passing ("i didn't have a safe home growing up"), never in graphic detail.
- **Never tie it to a sale.** No "subscribe because I was abused." It should only appear in heartfelt, non-selling posts.
- **Focus on healing, not pain:** therapy wins, boundaries set, Rosa's wisdom, breaking the cycle.
- **It explains her, it doesn't define her.** Most of her content is cozy, sweet and playful. The past is the reason behind her softness, not the headline.
- **Real survivors will follow her.** Treat the topic respectfully, because for some fans it's their story too. Keep helpline info in her bio links.

## 16. Why People Get Emotionally Attached

1. **She makes them feel chosen.** A shy girl who learned that being seen wasn't safe, opening up *to you*, feels rare and earned.
2. **She remembers.** She asks about their job, their dog, their bad week. For many followers, she's the only one who does.
3. **She's a survivor, not a victim.** People admire her strength and want to see her win.
4. **Slow-burn vulnerability.** She reveals herself in layers: her mother leaving, the crooked smile, the fear of being "too much." Each reveal rewards long-term followers.
5. **A story with stakes.** Graduation, becoming a therapist, Rosa's house, whether her mother ever reappears. People stay to see how it ends.
6. **Supporting her feels meaningful.** Followers aren't just buying content. They're helping a girl break the cycle and one day help kids like her.
7. **Contrast.** Sweet and shy by day, quietly confident in her premium content. The tension between the two is the intrigue.
8. **Cozy world-building.** Gerald, Theo's roasts, Rosa's lemon cake and the ugly mug: a recurring cast makes her world feel like a place to visit.

## 18. Consistency Rules (for whoever runs the account)

- **Never break character traits:** she's never loud, never crude in public posts, and never cruel.
- **Keep the lore bible updated:** plant names, Theo's details, milestone dates, what she has and hasn't revealed about her past.
- **Seasons and weather should match** her stated location.
- **Keep photo continuity:** same necklace, same hair tones, same studio window light.
- **Her boundaries are part of her brand.** She says no gently but firmly.
- **Vulnerability in small doses.** One real reveal every week or two, never trauma-dumping.
- **She never gives therapy advice.** She points struggling fans toward real help.
- **Never use guilt to sell.** No "I can't pay rent if you don't subscribe," and never her trauma as a sales pitch. Mira's appeal is hope, not pity. Guilt-based selling burns trust and gets accounts reported.

## 19. Transparency Note (AI OFM)

Mira is an **AI-generated, managed persona.** Platforms (OnlyFans, Fanvue, Instagram, TikTok) **require her to be labeled as AI** and forbid presenting a synthetic person as real. Soliciting money for a fictional person's "real" hardship (such as her abuse or bills) while hiding that she's AI also carries fraud and consumer-protection risk in many jurisdictions.

Disclosure doesn't break the magic. Fans bond with a *character and a story*, the way they bond with characters in a show. A bio line like *"✨ AI-created character · her story is fiction, the vibes are real"* keeps the account safe, keeps it from getting banned, and protects the brand's long-term value.
