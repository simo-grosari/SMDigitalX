Storytelling Formula Cheat Sheet

Client As Hero

Your buyer is not supporting your brand story. Your brand supports their transformation.

My client wants to become the kind of person who can book premium clients with confidence.

Visible Problem

Show the cost of staying stuck: lost leads, weak trust, wasted effort, unclear value.

Right now they feel scattered because their message changes every time they write.

Guide With Method

Your expertise becomes the bridge from confusion to buyer confidence.

My method turns raw experience into a repeatable story-led funnel.

Plan And Proof

Give a simple sequence and concrete evidence that your promise is believable.

First clarify the audience, then build the promise, then convert it into page copy.

Action And Future

Invite the buyer into a specific next step and show the future they are choosing.

Book a strategy session and leave with the message your market remembers.
One-sentence formula: I help [hero] overcome [problem] through [method] so they can [future outcome].

Sales Page Storytelling Script

1 Hook

Lead with a precise result, a sharp buyer tension, or a belief-shifting promise.

2 Problem Acknowledgment

Mirror what your buyer has tried and why it has not created the conversion lift they expected.

3 Transformation Story

Show the before-to-after journey with emotional and practical outcomes.

4 Solution Framework

Name the process, show the steps, and make your expertise easy to trust.

5 Social Proof And CTA

Use proof that reduces risk, then invite one clear next action.

Industry-Specific Customization Examples

Virtual Assistant

Offer: operations support for booked-out consultants.

Before

"I provide admin support for entrepreneurs who need help with inboxes, calendars, and tasks."

Weakness: commodity service, no transformation, no clear buyer trigger.

After

"I help booked-out consultants reclaim 8+ hours a week by turning scattered admin into a calm client operations system."

Story angle: from reactive overload to protected delivery capacity.

Executive Coach

Offer: leadership coaching for newly promoted directors.

Before

"I coach leaders to communicate better and build confidence at work."

Weakness: broad outcome, low urgency, sounds similar to every coach.

After

"I help newly promoted directors lead high-stakes conversations with calm authority so they can earn trust faster in their first 90 days."

Story angle: from hidden uncertainty to visible executive presence.

Brand Designer

Offer: strategic identity design for premium service brands.

Before

"I create beautiful branding and logos for small businesses."

Weakness: aesthetic-only positioning, no business case, unclear value.

After

"I help premium service providers turn expert-level value into a visual identity that attracts higher-trust, higher-budget clients."

Story angle: from polished visuals to market confidence and pricing power.
